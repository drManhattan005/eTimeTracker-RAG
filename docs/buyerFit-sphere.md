# eTimeTracker Buyer Fit

eTimeTracker is positioned for organizations that need a connected system for attendance, leave, shifts, field operations, travel, expenses, approvals, and operational visibility. It is especially relevant where workforce activity happens across sites, shifts, branches, customer locations, or subsidiaries. [page:1]

## Workforce Operating Model

### Field and Distributed Workforces

eTimeTracker is a strong fit for organizations with employees working outside a single office, including field teams, site-based staff, mobile employees, and distributed operations. Its location tracking, geofencing, outdoor-duty, tour, and visit-validation capabilities are designed around field activity. [page:1]

#### Fit Signals

- Employees work at customer sites, project sites, branches, warehouses, or other offsite locations
- Managers need to confirm whether field staff checked in at the expected location
- Operations teams need live employee movement and visit context
- Employees regularly raise outdoor-duty or tour requests
- Manual verification of field visits consumes manager time
- The organization needs to link location activity with attendance and approvals

- Mobile attendance and field workflows
