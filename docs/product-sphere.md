# eTimeTracker Product

eTimeTracker is an enterprise workforce-management platform for attendance, leaves, shifts, travel, expenses, outdoor duty, tasks, approvals, and reporting. It brings employee actions, manager controls, location context, and operational reporting into one connected system. [page:1]

## Product-Level Workflow Examples

### Attendance Correction Workflow

A typical attendance workflow can begin with biometric or face-recognition punching, validate relevant device, policy, and location context, synchronize the punch data, detect an exception, and route the record through an override or correction workflow where required. [page:1]

### Field Visit Workflow

A field employee can raise an outdoor-duty request, check in at a site through a configured geofence, create a location-aware visit record, and allow a manager to validate the visit in the same connected operational workflow. [page:1]

### Leave Approval Workflow

An employee can submit a leave request, review balance information, track status in real time, and have the request move through a configured multi-level approval chain or delegated approval path. [page:1]

### Tour and Expense Workflow

An employee can submit a tour request with itinerary context, log expenses in one or more currencies, and use a unified approval pipeline for travel, expenses, and related operational requests. [page:1]

### Shift Assignment Workflow

HR or operations teams can define shift patterns, generate schedules or monthly calendars, assign patterns to employees or organizational units, apply effective dates, and preserve historical assignments. [page:1]
