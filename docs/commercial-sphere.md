# eTimeTracker Commercial

eTimeTracker uses a per-employee, per-month model for Starter and Business plans, while Enterprise pricing is custom and tailored to organizational scale. The site states that pricing has no hidden fees and is intended to scale as the team grows. [page:1]

## Pricing Overview

### Pricing Model

eTimeTracker offers three commercial tiers: Starter, Business, and Enterprise. Starter and Business have published monthly per-employee pricing, while Enterprise requires a custom quote. [page:1]

#### Commercial Details

- Starter: ₹99 per employee per month
- Business: ₹249 per employee per month
- Enterprise: Custom pricing tailored to scale
- Pricing is positioned as having no hidden fees
- Plans are designed to scale as the team grows
- Employee capacity and included capabilities vary by plan

- How much does eTimeTracker cost?

- Does eTimeTracker have hidden fees?
- What pricing plans does eTimeTracker offer?
- Is Enterprise pricing publicly listed?

### Plan Selection

Plan selection depends primarily on employee count, number of locations or branches, required modules, support requirements, and whether multi-tenant administration or custom integrations are needed. [page:1]

#### Selection Criteria

- Choose Starter for up to 100 employees, one location, attendance and leave requirements, and basic reporting
- Choose Business for up to 1,000 employees, shifts, tours, expenses, geofencing, face approvals, and multi-branch operations
- Choose Enterprise for unlimited employees, multi-tenant administration, custom integrations, dedicated account management, and an SLA guarantee
- Confirm commercial requirements when the buyer needs capabilities not described on the pricing section
